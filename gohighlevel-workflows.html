GoHighLevel workflows

The GoHighLevel workflows worth building first.

GoHighLevel's workflow builder can automate almost anything, which is exactly why people get lost in it. These are the automations that pay for themselves first, in roughly the order I build them.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

1. Speed to lead

Trigger: form submitted, lead from a portal or ad, or new contact created. Action: a personal-sounding text and email within seconds, an internal alert, and a task to call. This is the single biggest win for most businesses. See speed to lead.

2. Missed call text back

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text straight away ("Sorry I missed you, how can I help?"). It turns lost calls into conversations.

3. Appointment confirmations and reminders

Confirmation on booking, then reminders the day before and an hour before, by text and email. Reminders are one of the cheapest ways to reduce no-shows.

4. No-show recovery

If an appointment is marked no-show, the lead gets a friendly rebooking message and the opportunity moves back a stage instead of being forgotten.

5. Long-term nurture

Leads who aren't ready yet get useful, low-pressure messages for months: market updates, new listings, check-ins. Most sales happen after the fifth or sixth touch, long after most people stop following up.

6. Review and referral requests

After a closing or completed job, ask for a review and, a few weeks later, a referral. Past clients are your cheapest source of new business.

7. Database reactivation

A one-off campaign to old leads already in your CRM, inviting them to reply. See database reactivation.

How to keep workflows from breaking

  • Name workflows clearly and keep one job per workflow.
  • Add "stop on response" so people who reply get a human, not another automation.
  • Check the execution logs weekly for failed actions.
  • Test with your own number before switching a workflow on.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is the most important GoHighLevel workflow?

For most businesses it's speed to lead: replying to every new enquiry within seconds by text and email and creating a task to call. Missed call text back is a close second.

Can GoHighLevel workflows send WhatsApp messages?

GoHighLevel offers a WhatsApp integration, and availability and costs depend on your account and region. Check HighLevel's current documentation for your setup.

Why are my GoHighLevel workflows not sending texts?

The most common causes in the US are missing A2P 10DLC registration, a contact who opted out (DND), or a workflow that stopped on response. The execution log shows which step failed.
