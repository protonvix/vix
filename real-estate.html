Questions

Real estate questions.

I use Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or another CRM. Can you still help?

Yes. I can move your leads and follow up into GoHighLevel, or connect your lead sources so GoHighLevel handles the speed to lead and nurture while your agents keep the tools they know. The free audit maps the best option.

Can you connect Zillow, Realtor.com and my Facebook ads?

Yes. Portal and ad leads are routed into GoHighLevel, tagged by source and answered instantly. Exact steps depend on how each portal sends leads, which I check during the audit.

Is texting my leads in the US compliant?

I register your A2P 10DLC brand and campaign, add opt out handling and quiet hours to every campaign, and only text people who have enquired or opted in. Your compliance rules always come first.

Will you talk to my clients?

Only if you want me to. By default I build and manage the system and every message goes out in your name, approved by you. I never contact your clients on my own.

Do you make cold calls?

No. I focus on the systems that make sure no lead you already paid for is wasted: instant replies, follow up, booked showings and revival.

How fast can this be live?

Speed to lead and showing reminders are usually live within the first week. Nurture, revival and reporting follow in the first month.
