Real estate referral system

A referral system that asks at the right moment.

Most agents agree referrals are their best business, and most never ask in a consistent way. A referral system makes the ask automatic, well timed and easy to say yes to.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

When to ask

  • Right after a great moment: an accepted offer, keys handed over, or a thank-you message from the client.
  • After a review: someone who has just written a five-star review is already in the mood to recommend you.
  • On anniversaries: the one-year home anniversary is a natural, friendly time to check in.
  • When their friends are likely moving: spring market, new school year, end of a lease.

How to ask so it doesn't feel pushy

  • Be specific: "If you know anyone thinking of selling this year, I'd love to help them the way I helped you."
  • Make it easy: a link or a simple reply ("just reply with their name and I'll take it from there").
  • Thank every referral, whether or not it turns into a sale.
  • Check local rules on referral gifts or fees before offering anything.

The automated referral system in GoHighLevel

  1. Closing or completion: the opportunity moves to "Closed", which starts the past client journey.
  2. Week 1: thank-you message and review request.
  3. Week 3: a friendly referral ask, from you.
  4. Every few months: useful check-ins (home value update, local news).
  5. Anniversary: a personal message, a review reminder if they haven't left one, and a gentle referral ask.
  6. Referral received: the new lead gets an instant reply, and the referrer gets an automatic thank-you plus a task for you to call them.

Questions

Quick answers.

How do I ask past clients for referrals?

At a happy moment (closing, a review, a home anniversary), with a specific, easy ask and a simple way to reply. Then thank them every time.

Can referral requests be automated?

Yes. A CRM such as GoHighLevel can send review and referral requests at set times after closing, and thank referrers automatically, while you make the personal calls.

Can I give a gift for a real estate referral?

Rules differ by state and country, and some places restrict referral fees or gifts. Check your local regulations and brokerage policy first.
