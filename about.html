About Vix Automations

We help businesses sell more, on autopilot

Vix Automations was founded by Promise Felix to help businesses stop losing customers to slow replies, outdated websites and missed follow-ups. We work with clients in Nigeria, the UK and worldwide.

What we believe

Most businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. Customers enquire and nobody replies fast enough, websites look outdated so buyers don't trust them, and social media gets views that never turn into sales. We fix all three.

What we do

  • GoHighLevel setup and automation: CRM, pipelines, funnels, calendars, workflows, reviews and AI assistants, built and tested for you.
  • Website design and redesign: modern, fast websites that win trust at first glance, including interactive 3D experiences.
  • TikTok marketing: four quality videos a week, account management, and every lead sent to your CRM.
  • SaaS mode, migrations and support: white-label launches for agencies, moves from other CRMs, and monthly help.

How we work

  • Fixed price first. You know the full cost before any work starts.
  • You own everything. Your GoHighLevel account, data, website and content belong to you.
  • Documented and recorded. Every system comes with walkthrough videos and written instructions.
  • Direct access. You talk to the people doing the work, on WhatsApp, email or video call.

Honest by design

We're a young company, so we don't show fake reviews or borrowed client logos. Instead we offer founding clients a fixed price, fixes at no extra charge and a 20% discount, in return for an honest review once their system is live.
