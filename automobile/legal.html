Privacy policy

When you book a viewing or ask for a price, you share your name, phone number and, if you choose, your email address. We use these only to reply to you about the car you asked about and to arrange your visit.

  • We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.
  • Booking requests are sent through WhatsApp, which handles your message under its own privacy policy.
  • You can ask us at any time to delete the details you shared with us.

This website does not use advertising cookies. The embedded Google map may set cookies controlled by Google.

Terms of sale

  • A car is reserved only after an agreed deposit is received.
  • Photos on this website are for illustration. Inspect the actual car before you pay.
  • Every car is sold with its customs and ownership documents.
  • Delivery outside Lagos is quoted separately before payment.
