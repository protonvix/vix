Why Famous Automobile

Buy with your eyes open.

Buying a car in Lagos should not feel like a gamble. Here is what you get with every car that leaves our lot.

Full inspection

Engine, gearbox, suspension and body checked before any car goes on the floor. Ask and we show you the report.

Clean papers

Customs duty paid and documented. You leave with the papers you need to register and insure.

Verified history

For foreign used cars we pull the VIN history so you know about past accidents and mileage.

Flexible payment

Pay outright or spread it out. We work with partner banks on payment plans for qualifying buyers.

Trade in and swap

Bring your current car. We value it on the spot and take it off the price of your next one.

Delivery across Nigeria

Buying from Abuja, Port Harcourt or Ibadan? We deliver to your door, with the car insured on the way.

Questions

Before you come in

Are your cars brand new or foreign used?

Both. We stock brand new cars and carefully selected foreign used (Tokunbo) cars. Each listing in the showroom tells you which one it is.

Is customs duty already paid?

Yes. Every car we sell is duty cleared, and you receive the customs papers with your purchase documents.

Can I bring my own mechanic to inspect the car?

Please do. Book a viewing and bring whoever you trust. We will lift the car and give them time to check everything.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for qualifying buyers through our partner banks. Send us the car you want and we will explain the deposit and monthly options.

Can I swap my current car?

Yes. Bring it in or send photos on WhatsApp. We give you a valuation and take it off the price of your next car.

Why do listings say "Ask for price"?

Prices move with the exchange rate. Message us and we give you today's exact price for the car, with nothing hidden.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. We deliver to any state in Nigeria. Delivery cost depends on distance and we quote it before you pay.

The car I want is not listed. Can you find it?

Yes. Tell us the make, model, year and colour. We source it and keep you updated until it arrives.
