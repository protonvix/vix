Pricing FAQ

Good to know

Which currency will I pay in?

Packages are priced in US dollars. Other currencies are shown as an estimate using a recent exchange rate, so you can see roughly what you’ll pay. Your invoice shows the exact amount before you pay anything.

How does the founding-client discount work?

I’m building my client base, so early clients get a discount on any package. In return I ask for an honest review and permission to share your results once your system is live.

What does “fixed price” mean?

We agree the scope in writing before any work starts, and that’s the price you pay. If you want extra work later, I quote it separately first. Nothing is added without your approval.

Is the HighLevel subscription included?

No. You pay HighLevel directly for your own account, so you always own it. My packages cover the setup, build and training.
