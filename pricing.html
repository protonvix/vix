Questions

Good to know.

Is the GoHighLevel subscription included?

No. You pay HighLevel directly for your own account, so you always own it. My prices cover the setup, the work and the support.

Which currency will I pay in?

Prices are set in US dollars. Other currencies are shown as an estimate, and your invoice shows the exact amount before you pay.

How can I pay?

By card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack, or by bank transfer to accounts in the US, UK, Europe and Nigeria.

What does fixed price mean?

The scope is agreed in writing before work starts, and that is the price. Anything extra is quoted first and only added with your approval.

Monthly plan or one off build?

If your account needs building, start with a one off build. If it is set up but needs ongoing work, a monthly plan fits better. The free audit tells you which.
