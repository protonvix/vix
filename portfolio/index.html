FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Send me a message and I will answer personally.

Is the website audit really free?

Yes. You receive a clear summary of what is working, what is holding your website back and what to fix first. There is no obligation to hire me.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes a few days. A full business website typically takes one to three weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content is ready. You receive a delivery date before work begins.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is priced individually based on pages and features. You receive a fixed quote before any work begins, and the price only changes if the scope changes.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Once your project is paid for, your domain, website files, images and content belong to you. There is no lock in.

How does payment work?

Projects usually start with a deposit, with the balance due before launch. The exact terms are agreed in writing at the start.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work with businesses in Nigeria, the United Kingdom and internationally, with clear communication by call, email and WhatsApp.

Can you redesign a site built on WordPress, Wix or GoHighLevel?

Yes. I can redesign on your current platform or recommend a faster setup if it serves your business better.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Every website is designed for phones first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. I remain available for updates, fixes and new pages after your website goes live.
