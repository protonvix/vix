Yes. Send your link and I check speed, mobile layout, SEO and how easy it is to contact you. You get the findings on WhatsApp with no obligation.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes a few days. A full business website takes one to three weeks, depending on pages and how quickly content is ready.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. Send your details through the form and I reply with a fixed price before any work starts.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work with clients in Nigeria, the UK and anywhere else. Everything happens over WhatsApp, calls and email.

Can you redesign a site built on WordPress, Wix or GoHighLevel?

Yes. I can redesign on the same platform or move you to a faster setup if that serves you better.

Will my website work well on phones?

Every site I build is designed for phones first, because that is where most of your customers will see it.

Yes. I stay available for updates, fixes and new pages after your site goes live.
