How it works

How to use the Vix Automations website

A quick, step-by-step tour: find the right service, check prices in your currency, ask questions, and get started. Each step has a Try it button that takes you straight there.

1. Menu 2. Services 3. Service pages 4. Prices 5. Ask a question 6. Get a quote 7. Book a call 8. Pay What happens next
1

Find your way around

The menu at the top of every page takes you anywhere on the site.

  • Services: everything we offer, on the home page
  • TikTok Marketing and Web Design: full pages for each service
  • Pricing: package prices in your currency
  • Book a Strategy Call: the fastest way to talk to us

On a phone? Tap the ☰ menu icon at the top right to open the same menu.

Try it: open the home page
2

See all our services

Scroll down the home page, or tap Services, to see everything we do in one place: GoHighLevel setup and automation, website design and fixes, TikTok marketing, and ongoing support.

Each card explains the service in a sentence or two. Cards with a link take you to a page with full details.

Try it: view services
3

Open a service page

Each main service has its own page with what's included, how the process works, packages and answers to common questions.

  • Home page: GoHighLevel CRM, funnels and automation
  • Web Design: new websites, redesigns, website fixes and GoHighLevel sites
  • TikTok Marketing: 4 videos a week, account and engagement management

The page you're on is highlighted in the menu, and the main button near the top starts your request.

Web Design TikTok Marketing
4

Check prices in your currency

The Pricing page shows our GoHighLevel packages with the current founding-client discount already applied.

  • Prices appear in your local currency automatically, based on where you are.
  • Use the Currency menu to switch, for example to naira, pounds, euros or dollars. The site remembers your choice.
  • Prices are set in US dollars. Other currencies are estimates, and your invoice shows the exact amount.

Web design and TikTok packages are priced by custom quote, because every project is different.

Try it: see pricing
5

Ask a question anytime

Two round buttons sit in the bottom-right corner of every page.

  • Chat button (blue): opens our assistant. Type a question or tap a topic like Pricing, Services or How to pay for an instant answer.
  • WhatsApp button (green): opens a chat with a real person on WhatsApp.

The assistant is automated. If it can't answer, it gives you a button to send your exact question to us on WhatsApp.

6

Request a custom quote

On the Web Design page, choose a package and fill in the short quote form.

  • Tap Get My Custom Quote on a package and it's selected in the form for you.
  • Tick the features you want, then add your budget, timeline, name and email.
  • Send it with Send via WhatsApp or Send via Email. Your answers are filled in for you, so you just press send.
Try it: open the quote form
7

Book a free strategy call

At the bottom of the home page, fill in the contact form: your name, email, the service you need and a few words about your project.

Press Request My Strategy Call and your email app opens with your message ready to send. We reply within 1 business day to arrange a time.

The same section lists our email, WhatsApp number and LinkedIn if you'd rather reach out directly.

Try it: book a call
8

Pay the way that suits you

Once you've approved your quote, you'll get an invoice with payment details. We accept:

  • Payoneer
  • Cleva
  • Wise
  • Bank transfer to accounts in Nigeria, the UK and the USA
Try it: see payment options

After you get in touch

What happens next

From first message to finished project, here's what to expect.

  1. We replyUsually within 1 business day, by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Free strategy callWe learn about your business and goals, and answer your questions.
  3. Fixed quote in writingThe scope and price are agreed before any work starts.
  4. Approve & payYou approve the quote and pay using the invoice details.
  5. We buildYou see designs and progress along the way and can request changes.
  6. Launch & trainingWe go live together and you get recorded walkthroughs for your team.
