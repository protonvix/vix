A2P 10DLC

Texts not sending? Start with A2P 10DLC.

If you text US numbers from GoHighLevel using a regular 10-digit number, US carriers expect your business and messaging to be registered. Skip it and your texts can be filtered, blocked or charged extra.

What A2P 10DLC means

A2P is application-to-person messaging (texts sent from software). 10DLC is a standard 10-digit long code phone number. US mobile carriers require businesses sending A2P texts on these numbers to register a brand (your business) and a campaign (what you send and why).

How registration works in GoHighLevel

  1. Make sure your business details match your official registration exactly (legal name, EIN, address, website).
  2. Submit the brand registration in your phone settings.
  3. Submit a campaign describing your use case, with sample messages and how people opt in.
  4. Show opt-in on your website forms and add opt-out wording to your messages.
  5. Wait for approval. It can take days, sometimes longer if anything is rejected.

Why registrations get rejected

  • Business details that don't match official records.
  • A website with no privacy policy or no visible opt-in wording.
  • Sample messages that don't match the use case or don't include opt-out language.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do I need A2P 10DLC in GoHighLevel?

If you send texts to US numbers from a standard 10-digit US number, yes. Unregistered traffic is likely to be filtered or blocked by carriers.

Does A2P 10DLC apply in the UK?

No. A2P 10DLC is a US carrier requirement. UK messaging has its own rules, mainly consent under PECR and UK GDPR.

How long does A2P 10DLC approval take?

Often a few days, sometimes longer, especially if a submission is rejected and needs changes.
