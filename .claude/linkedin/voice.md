## My positions

1. A chatbot is not a sales system. Replying is not the same as following up and closing.
2. Clients should never have to ask "any update?". If they do, the process failed, not the client.
3. Anyone can design a website now. What wins is everything after the click: follow-up, booking, payment.
4. Build in public and be honest. Don't fake numbers, results or reviews.
