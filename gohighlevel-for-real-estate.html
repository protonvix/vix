GoHighLevel for real estate

GoHighLevel for realtors and estate agents.

Most agents don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. GoHighLevel can fix it, if it's set up for how property is actually bought, sold and let.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

What GoHighLevel does for a real estate business

  • Answers every enquiry fast from your website, portals (Zillow, Realtor.com, Rightmove, Zoopla) and ads.
  • Books showings (US) and viewings (UK) straight into your calendar with reminders.
  • Nurtures buyers and sellers who are months away, so you're the agent they remember.
  • Keeps past clients close with check-ins, anniversaries and market updates that bring referrals.
  • Collects reviews after every closing or completion.
  • Shows which lead sources make money, so you spend where it works.

US realtors

US setups need A2P 10DLC registration before texting at scale (see A2P 10DLC guide), plus quiet hours and opt-out handling. Many teams run GoHighLevel alongside an existing CRM like Follow Up Boss or kvCORE for speed to lead and nurture (see GoHighLevel vs Follow Up Boss).

UK estate and letting agents

UK setups use UK phone numbers and consent-based messaging under PECR and UK GDPR. Portal enquiries from Rightmove and Zoopla can be routed into GoHighLevel so every applicant gets a fast reply and a viewing link. See GoHighLevel in the UK.

Investors and wholesalers

Seller leads from direct mail, PPC and cold calling need relentless, compliant follow-up. Separate seller pipelines, offer follow-up and buyer lists for dispositions keep deals moving.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is GoHighLevel good for real estate agents?

Yes, when it's set up for real estate. It handles speed to lead, booking, nurture, past client marketing and reviews in one place. Out of the box it's generic, so the setup is what makes it work.

Can GoHighLevel connect to Zillow or Rightmove?

Portal leads usually arrive by email or through an integration. They can be routed into GoHighLevel so each enquiry triggers an instant reply and lands in the right pipeline. The exact method depends on the portal and your plan.

Does GoHighLevel have an IDX website?

GoHighLevel builds websites and funnels, but property search (IDX in the US) usually comes from a separate provider embedded on the site. I can connect the two so enquiries flow into your pipeline.
