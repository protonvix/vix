GoHighLevel vs Follow Up Boss

GoHighLevel vs Follow Up Boss: an honest comparison.

Both are popular with real estate teams, and they're built for different jobs. Here's how they compare, and why many teams end up using both.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

Follow Up Boss in short

Follow Up Boss is a CRM built specifically for real estate. Teams like its lead routing, agent accountability, simple interface and the large number of real estate lead sources it connects to. Pricing is generally per user, so costs grow with team size. Check Follow Up Boss for current plans.

GoHighLevel in short

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one platform: CRM, pipelines, workflows, websites and funnels, calendars, email and SMS, reviews and AI. It isn't real estate specific, so it needs proper setup, but it can replace several tools at once. HighLevel's plans are priced per account rather than per user; check HighLevel for current pricing.

Side by side

  • Real estate lead sources: Follow Up Boss is stronger out of the box.
  • Automation and nurture: GoHighLevel's workflow builder is more flexible.
  • Websites, funnels and booking: built into GoHighLevel; separate tools with Follow Up Boss.
  • Ease of use for agents: Follow Up Boss is simpler; GoHighLevel depends on how it's set up.
  • Cost as you grow: per-user pricing adds up; account-based pricing usually doesn't.

Running both together

Many teams keep Follow Up Boss for agents and lead routing and use GoHighLevel for speed to lead, long-term nurture, past client marketing and reviews. The key is a clean connection so contacts don't get messaged twice.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is GoHighLevel better than Follow Up Boss?

Neither is better for everyone. Follow Up Boss is simpler and built for real estate; GoHighLevel is more flexible and replaces more tools. Many teams use both.

Can I move from Follow Up Boss to GoHighLevel?

Yes. Contacts, tags and notes can be exported and imported, and pipelines and automations rebuilt in GoHighLevel. Plan the move so follow-up doesn't stop during the switch.
