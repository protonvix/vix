Past client retention

Keep past clients coming back, not going to competitors.

Your past clients already trust you. Yet many will use a different agent next time, simply because nobody stayed in touch. Past client marketing fixes that, and it's the cheapest growth most agents ignore.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

Why past clients drift away

  • Contact stops the day after closing or completion.
  • Generic newsletters get ignored or unsubscribed.
  • Years later, they can't remember your name, but they remember the agent who sent them a home value update last month.

A past client plan that works

  • Right after closing: thank you, review request, and helpful moving-in tips.
  • Every one to two months: something genuinely useful: an estimate of their home's value, local market news, maintenance reminders, a trusted tradesperson list.
  • Home anniversary: a personal message (and a small, permitted gesture if you like).
  • Personal moments: birthdays, a new baby, kids starting school, where they've shared them.
  • Twice a year: a personal call or voice note. Automation reminds you; you make the call.
  • Regular referral asks at the right moments (see referral system).

Signs a past client might move again

  • They click on home value updates or listings in their area.
  • They've been in the home for several years and their family has changed.
  • They reply to check-ins with questions about the market.

GoHighLevel can tag these signals and create a task for you to reach out personally, at exactly the right time.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is past client marketing in real estate?

Staying in useful, regular contact with people you've helped buy, sell or let, so they come back to you and refer friends instead of choosing another agent.

How often should I contact past clients?

Something useful every one to two months, plus personal moments like home anniversaries, and a personal call or voice note a couple of times a year.

Can past client marketing be automated?

Most of it can, with a CRM such as GoHighLevel: useful updates, anniversary messages, review and referral requests, and alerts for when to call personally.
