Speed to lead

The first agent to reply usually wins.

Speed to lead is how quickly you respond to a new enquiry. In real estate, it often decides who gets the client, and most agents are far slower than they think.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

What the numbers say

Several industry sources cite an Inman survey reporting that the average agent takes around 15 hours to respond to a new online lead (HousingWire, Luxury Presence). Luxury Presence also reports an analysis of US residential leads in which leads reached within five minutes were contacted live about four times as often as those first contacted after 30 minutes. Exact figures vary by study, but the direction is always the same: faster wins.

Why agents are slow

  • Leads arrive at night, at weekends and during showings or viewings.
  • Portal emails get buried in a busy inbox.
  • There's no system to alert the right person instantly.

How to reply in seconds with GoHighLevel

  1. Route every lead source (website, portals, ads) into GoHighLevel.
  2. Send an instant, personal text and email that asks one easy question.
  3. Alert the agent by app notification and create a call task.
  4. Offer a booking link so ready leads can pick a time themselves.
  5. If nobody replies, follow up automatically for days, then weeks.
  6. Use Conversation AI or Voice AI out of hours, with clear handover rules.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is speed to lead?

The time between a lead enquiring and your first meaningful response. Shorter is better: the first business to respond usually gets the conversation.

How fast should I respond to a real estate lead?

Within five minutes wherever possible, and an automatic personal reply within seconds so the lead knows you're on it.

Can I automate speed to lead without sounding like a robot?

Yes. Short, personal messages that ask one simple question work far better than long templates. A human then takes over the conversation.
