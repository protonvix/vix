Privacy policy

This website belongs to Kudam Islamic Model School (KIMS), Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria. We respect your privacy and follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

When you send an admission enquiry, the details you type (your name, phone number, optional email, your child's name, the class and any note) are placed into a WhatsApp message. Nothing is stored on this website. The message only reaches the school when you press send in WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a school visit.
  • To process your child's admission if you choose to enrol.

We never sell or share your details with anyone outside the school.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent. Contact the school office to make a request.

Terms of use

The content of this website, including the school name, badge and photographs, belongs to Kudam Islamic Model School and may not be reused without permission. Fees, dates and programmes shown here can change; the school office will always confirm the current details.
