Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

Which classes do you admit?

We admit pupils into creche, nursery, primary 1 to 6, JSS 1 to 3 and SS 1 to 2. Entry into SS3 is considered case by case.

Do you follow the Nigerian national curriculum?

Yes. We teach the full national curriculum and prepare students for BECE, WAEC, NECO and JAMB, with Qur'an, Arabic and Islamic Religious Studies added to the timetable.

How is Qur'an memorisation taught?

Pupils have a Qur'an period every school day. Teachers follow a structured plan beginning with Juz Amma, and progress is tracked and shared with parents each term.

Can my child join in the middle of a session?

Yes, if there is space in the class. Your child will sit a short assessment so we can support them to settle in quickly.

What is the school uniform?

Girls wear a dark green hijab and a long dark green gown. Boys wear a white shirt, dark green trousers and a green cap. Uniforms are available from the school.

How much are the school fees?

Fees depend on the class. Send us an enquiry or call the school office and we will share the current fee schedule and payment options.

How do you keep parents informed?

Parents receive termly report cards, invitations to open days and PTA meetings, and can reach class teachers through the school office.

Is the school safe?

Our campus is fully fenced and gated, staff supervise pupils throughout the day, and children are only released to known parents or guardians.
