GoHighLevel cost

What GoHighLevel really costs.

The subscription is only part of the price. Here's the full picture: the platform, usage charges, and setup, so you can budget properly.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

1. The platform subscription

At the time of writing, HighLevel lists plans starting at $97 a month, with higher tiers for unlimited sub-accounts and agency features. Prices change, so check HighLevel's pricing page for current figures.

2. Usage charges

  • Phone and SMS: charged per call minute and per message, plus phone number fees.
  • Email: sending through HighLevel's email service is usage based.
  • AI: Conversation AI, Voice AI and the AI Employee are paid add-ons or usage based.
  • US texting registration: A2P 10DLC has registration and carrier fees.

3. Setup and support

Industry guides quote a wide range for professional setup, from a few hundred dollars for a focused setup to several thousand for a full multi-pipeline build. At Vix Assist:

  • One-off build or rescue: from $499, fixed price.
  • Monthly plans (setup plus ongoing management): $300 (Lead Response), $500 (Growth) and $670 (Brokerage) a month, with no long contract.
  • Free revenue audit and written plan before you commit.

Is it worth it?

For a real estate agent, one extra closing a year usually covers the whole cost many times over. The real question is whether the account will actually be set up and looked after. See the pricing page or book a free strategy call.
