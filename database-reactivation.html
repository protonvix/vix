Database reactivation

Your next client may already be in your CRM.

Database reactivation means contacting old leads who never went anywhere and inviting them back into a conversation. It's often the fastest return in GoHighLevel because the leads are already paid for.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

How a reactivation campaign works

  1. Clean the list. Remove opt-outs, duplicates, wrong numbers and anyone who asked not to be contacted.
  2. Check consent. Only message people who enquired or opted in, and follow local rules (TCPA and A2P 10DLC in the US; PECR and UK GDPR in the UK).
  3. Send a short, human message that asks one easy question, for example: "Hi Sam, are you still thinking about moving this year?"
  4. Send in small batches so you can handle replies and protect your number's reputation.
  5. Route replies to the right pipeline stage with a task to call.
  6. Follow up once or twice, then move non-responders to long-term nurture.

What to expect

Results depend on how old the list is, how the leads were collected and how good the follow-up is. The value is that every reply costs almost nothing compared with buying new leads.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Blasting thousands of contacts at once.
  • Long, salesy messages with links in the first text.
  • No plan for handling replies quickly.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is database reactivation?

A campaign that re-engages old leads already in your CRM, usually by text and email, to start new conversations without buying new leads.

Is database reactivation legal?

It can be, when you only message people who enquired or opted in, honour opt-outs, and follow local rules such as TCPA and A2P 10DLC in the US or PECR and UK GDPR in the UK. This isn't legal advice; check your own obligations.
