Best CRM for real estate teams

The best CRM for your real estate team is the one agents actually use.

There's no single best real estate CRM. There's the one that fits how your team gets leads, follows up and keeps past clients. Here's how to choose without wasting months on the wrong one.

What actually matters

  • Lead routing: new leads reach the right agent instantly, with a backup if they don't respond.
  • Speed to lead automation: an instant reply by text and email, before anyone picks up the phone.
  • Long-term nurture: follow-up that runs for months without agents remembering to do it.
  • Past client marketing: check-ins, anniversaries and review and referral requests.
  • Agent adoption: a simple mobile app agents will really open.
  • Reporting: which lead sources and agents turn into closings.
  • Cost as you grow: per-user pricing versus account-based pricing.

The main options

  • Follow Up Boss: a real estate CRM known for lead routing, agent accountability and many lead source integrations.
  • BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE): CRM plus IDX websites and marketing, popular with brokerages.
  • Lofty (formerly Chime): CRM, IDX websites and AI tools in one platform.
  • GoHighLevel: an all-in-one platform (CRM, workflows, websites, booking, reviews, AI) that isn't real estate specific, so it needs a proper setup, but it's very flexible.
  • UK agency software (for example Reapit, Alto or Street): property and agency management for UK estate and letting agents, often paired with a separate tool for marketing and follow-up.

Features and pricing change often, so check each provider for current details.

Why many teams combine tools

A common setup is a real estate CRM or agency system for agents and transactions, plus GoHighLevel for speed to lead, nurture, past client retention and reviews. It works well as long as the connection is clean and nobody gets messaged twice. See GoHighLevel vs Follow Up Boss.

A quick way to decide

  1. Write down where your leads come from and how many a month.
  2. Time how long your team takes to reply today.
  3. List what happens to a lead who isn't ready for six months.
  4. List what happens to a client after closing or completion.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is the best CRM for a real estate team?

It depends on how your team works. Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail and Lofty are built for real estate; GoHighLevel is more flexible and covers marketing, booking and reviews. Many teams use a real estate CRM with GoHighLevel for follow-up.

Is GoHighLevel a good CRM for real estate brokers?

Yes, when it's set up for real estate: pipelines for buyers, sellers and past clients, instant replies, nurture and review requests. Out of the box it's generic.

What CRM do UK estate agents use?

Many use agency software such as Reapit, Alto or Street for listings and transactions, and add a marketing and follow-up tool such as GoHighLevel for fast replies and past client marketing.
