Web design FAQ

Questions, answered

Why is every package a custom quote?

Websites vary a lot: number of pages, features, content and platform. We quote each project on its real scope, so you never pay for things you don’t need. The quote is fixed before work starts.

Can you fix my website even if you didn’t build it?

Yes. We fix websites built with custom code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React) and no-code builders like WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and GoHighLevel.

Can you build my website inside GoHighLevel?

Yes. We build websites, funnels and landing pages in GoHighLevel, connect your forms, calendars and automations, and can move your existing site over.

How long does a website take?

Most fixes take 1–3 days. Starter websites usually take 1–2 weeks, and larger sites 3–6 weeks depending on pages and features. Your quote includes a timeline.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your website, content, domain and design files belong to you.

How do I pay?

Payoneer, Cleva, Wise, or bank transfer to accounts in Nigeria, the UK and the USA. Payment details are on your invoice.
