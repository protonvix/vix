What a GoHighLevel expert actually does

GoHighLevel can replace a CRM, email tool, booking app, website builder and review platform in one account. That power is also why so many accounts end up half set up: pipelines with no automations, forms that don't trigger anything, and texts that never send because compliance was skipped.

A GoHighLevel expert turns the account into a working system. For most clients that means:

  • Pipelines that match how you really sell, with clear stages and owners.
  • Workflows that reply to new leads in seconds, follow up for weeks and book appointments automatically.
  • Calendars with reminders that cut no-shows.
  • Forms, funnels and websites connected to the right pipeline stage.
  • Phone and SMS set up correctly, including A2P 10DLC registration in the US.
  • AI agents (Conversation AI, Voice AI, the AI Employee) configured with sensible guardrails.
  • Reporting so you can see which lead sources turn into revenue.

Who this service is for

Real estate brokers, teams, agents and investors are the main focus, in the US (realtors) and the UK (estate and letting agents). The same systems also work for home services, clinics, coaches and agencies who want leads answered fast and followed up properly.

How it works

  1. Free audit. I review your current account or lead process and send a written plan.
  2. Fixed quote. You see the full price before anything starts.
  3. Build and test. Every workflow is tested end to end on desktop and mobile.
  4. Handover. Walkthrough videos, written notes and access you control.
  5. Optional monthly support to keep improving follow-up and reporting.

What to check before you hire anyone

  • Do they give a written scope and fixed price?
  • Will they work in your account, so you keep ownership?
  • Do they handle SMS compliance (A2P 10DLC in the US, consent rules in the UK)?
  • Do they test workflows and hand over documentation?

If the answer is yes to all four, you're in good hands, whoever you choose. To talk through your setup, book a free strategy call.
