GoHighLevel pipelines

GoHighLevel pipelines that match how you really sell.

A pipeline in GoHighLevel is the list of stages a lead moves through, from first enquiry to closed deal. Set it up well and you always know who to call next. Set it up badly and leads go quiet without anyone noticing.

The rules of a good pipeline

  • One pipeline per journey, not per lead source. Buyers and sellers need different stages; Zillow and Rightmove leads don't.
  • Five to eight stages. More than that and nobody keeps them up to date.
  • Every stage has an action. A stage with no task or automation attached is where deals go to die.
  • Use tags and custom fields for detail, not extra stages (budget, area, timeline, source).
  • Automate the moves you can: booking a call, missing an appointment or signing a contract should move the opportunity for you.

Example: a real estate buyer pipeline

  1. New lead: instant text and email; task to call within five minutes.
  2. Contacted: two-way conversation started.
  3. Qualified: budget, area, timeline and financing confirmed.
  4. Showing booked: reminders sent automatically.
  5. Offer made: follow-up tasks and updates.
  6. Under contract (US) or sale agreed (UK).
  7. Closed / completed: review request and referral ask go out.
  8. Nurture: not ready yet; long-term follow-up keeps them warm.

Sellers, landlords and past clients get their own pipelines with the same discipline. The past client pipeline is the one most agents skip, and it's where repeat business and referrals come from.

Common pipeline mistakes

  • Leads sitting in "New" for days because no workflow creates a task.
  • Deals marked lost when they were really "not yet", so follow-up stops.
  • Opportunity values left empty, so reporting can't show revenue by source.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is a pipeline in GoHighLevel?

A set of stages (for example New lead, Qualified, Appointment booked, Won) that opportunities move through. It shows where every deal is and can trigger automations when a deal changes stage.

How many pipelines should I have in GoHighLevel?

Usually one per customer journey. A real estate team might have Buyers, Sellers, Landlords or Investors, and Past clients. Lead source belongs in a tag or field, not a separate pipeline.

Can GoHighLevel move opportunities automatically?

Yes. Workflows can create or move opportunities when a form is submitted, an appointment is booked or missed, a tag is added, or a reply comes in.
