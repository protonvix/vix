What a GoHighLevel virtual assistant does each week

  • Pipeline hygiene: moves opportunities to the right stage, removes duplicates and flags deals that have gone quiet.
  • Lead follow-up: checks that every new lead got a reply and that nurture sequences are running.
  • Workflow health: spots failed actions, broken triggers and texts that didn't send, and fixes them.
  • Calendars: keeps availability, reminders and round-robin rules up to date.
  • Content updates: refreshes email and SMS templates, snippets and forms.
  • Reporting: a short weekly summary of leads, appointments and where deals are stuck.
  • Reviews and referrals: makes sure review requests go out after every closing or job.

Assistant or expert: which do you need?

If your account isn't built yet, start with a setup (see GoHighLevel expert setup). If it's built but nobody has time to look after it, an ongoing assistant is the better fit. Most real estate teams need both: a proper build, then weekly care so the system keeps paying off.
