Our treatments

Every treatment starts with an exam and a clear plan, including the cost, so you can decide with confidence.

Check-ups and cleaning

Regular visits keep small problems small. We check your teeth, gums and bite, then clean away the build-up that brushing misses.

  • Full dental exam
  • Scaling and polishing
  • Advice on care at home

Braces and aligners

Straighten crooked or crowded teeth and correct your bite, at any age. We help you choose between traditional braces and clear aligners.

  • Metal and ceramic braces
  • Clear aligners
  • Retainers after treatment

Dental implants

An implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. It looks, feels and works like a natural tooth.

  • Single tooth implants
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • Assessment to check you're a good fit

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening lifts stains safely, under a dentist's care.

  • In-clinic whitening
  • Take-home whitening kits

Root canal treatment

When infection reaches the inside of a tooth, root canal treatment removes it, eases the pain and saves the tooth.

  • Treatment to ease the pain
  • Crown to protect the tooth afterwards

Smile makeovers

Change the shape, colour or size of your teeth with a plan built around the smile you want.

  • Veneers
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Composite bonding

Children's dentistry

Gentle first visits that help children feel at ease with the dentist.

  • Check-ups and cleaning
  • Fissure sealants and fluoride

Patients visiting from abroad are welcome. Send us a message before you travel, and we'll arrange your visits to fit your stay.
