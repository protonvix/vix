Real estate lead follow-up

How to follow up with real estate leads until they're ready.

Most real estate leads aren't ready to move today. The agent who wins them is the one who's still in touch, helpfully, when they are. Here's a follow-up system you can run by hand or automate.

The first five minutes

  • Reply by text and email straight away with one easy question ("Is it the 3-bed on Oak Street you'd like to see, or are you still browsing?").
  • Call if you can. If they don't answer, leave a short voicemail and send a text saying you called.
  • Offer a booking link so ready leads can pick a showing or viewing time themselves.

Speed matters more than polish here. See speed to lead for why.

The first week

  • Day 1: a second text later the same day if there's no reply.
  • Day 2: a call, then a helpful email (similar homes, area guide, next steps).
  • Day 4: a short check-in text.
  • Day 7: a "should I keep sending you homes?" message. It's honest and gets surprisingly many replies.

The next twelve months: the nurture sequence

Leads who go quiet aren't lost; they're early. Keep in touch with something useful roughly every two to four weeks:

  • New listings that match what they asked for.
  • A monthly market update for their area.
  • Buying or selling tips at the right moment (mortgage rates, school terms, seasonal timing).
  • A personal check-in every couple of months, from you, not a template.

Rules that keep follow-up from feeling like spam

  • Stop automated messages the moment someone replies, and let a human take over.
  • Keep texts short and personal. No links in the very first text.
  • Always make it easy to opt out, and respect quiet hours.
  • Follow local rules: A2P 10DLC and consent in the US, PECR and UK GDPR in the UK.

Questions

Quick answers.

How many times should you follow up with a real estate lead?

Far more than most agents do. Several touches in the first week, then useful contact every few weeks for months. Many leads only become active long after their first enquiry.

What should I text a new real estate lead?

Something short and personal with one easy question, for example: "Hi Sam, it's Alex from Harbor Realty. Is it the 3-bed on Oak Street you'd like to see, or are you still browsing?"

When should I stop following up with a lead?

When they ask you to stop, buy elsewhere, or clearly aren't moving. Otherwise move them to long-term nurture rather than deleting them.
