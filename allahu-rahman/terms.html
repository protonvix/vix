Terms of sale

Last updated November 2026

Orders

Sending your bag to us on WhatsApp is a request to order. Your order is confirmed once we reply with availability, the delivery fee and the final total.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Naira. We may update prices when supplier costs change; the price we confirm in the chat is the price you pay. Payment is by bank transfer to the account we send you in the chat. Never pay into an account shared anywhere else.

Delivery

Delivery fees and timelines depend on your location and are confirmed before you pay. Please make sure your phone is reachable on the delivery day.

Returns and exchanges

  • If an item arrives damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered, message us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos and we will arrange a replacement or refund.
  • Size exchanges are possible when the item is unworn, in its original packaging and the size you need is in stock. The customer covers delivery for size exchanges.
  • For hygiene reasons, worn footwear, opened baby items and jewellery cannot be returned unless faulty.

Contact

For any question about an order, message us on WhatsApp from the store home page.
