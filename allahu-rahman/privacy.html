Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not store your details on a server. When you place an order we receive the information you choose to send us on WhatsApp, such as your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you want.

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order or a return
  • To send new arrival updates, only if you ask to join

Your bag and wishlist

Items in your bag and wishlist are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you come back. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We only share your name, phone number and address with the delivery partner taking your parcel to you.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to delete your chat history and details, or to stop sending you updates. Just send us a message on WhatsApp.
