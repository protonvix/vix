How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag, open the bag and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". Your list, sizes, colours and total go straight to our orders line (0816 427 4103), and we reply to confirm stock and delivery.

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

Delivery cost depends on your location and the size of the order. We tell you the exact fee before you pay, along with the expected delivery date.

How do I pay?

Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp we send our account details for bank transfer. Please only pay into the account we send you in the chat.

How do I know my size?

Shoes and slippers use EU sizes (for example 38 or 41). Tell customer care (0703 312 1920) your usual size or foot length and we will recommend the best fit for that style.

Are the colours in the photos accurate?

We photograph our stock as closely to real life as we can. If you want to be sure, ask us for a live photo or video of the exact item before you pay.

Can I buy in bulk for resale?

Yes. Many of our slippers and sandals are available by the dozen. Message our orders line (0816 427 4103) with the item and quantity for a wholesale price.

What if an item arrives damaged or wrong?

Message customer care (0703 312 1920) within 48 hours of delivery with photos. See our returns policy for full details.
