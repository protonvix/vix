TikTok FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be on camera?

No. We can make faceless content (voiceovers, text-on-screen, product and screen-recorded videos), work with UGC creators, or edit clips you film yourself. We’ll recommend what works best for your niche.

Can you guarantee a number of followers or views?

No one can honestly guarantee TikTok numbers, because the algorithm decides who sees each video. What we guarantee is the work: 4 quality videos every week, daily engagement, and a clear monthly report. Consistent, quality posting is what grows accounts.

Which niches do you work with?

Most service businesses and brands: beauty and med spas, coaching, real estate, home services, e-commerce, restaurants, fitness, agencies and more. We research your niche before creating anything.

How soon will I see results?

Most accounts need 4–8 weeks of consistent posting before growth picks up. Some videos take off sooner. We track what works and do more of it every month.

Do I approve videos before they’re posted?

Yes. You approve the monthly content plan and can review each video before it goes live.

How do I pay?

Payoneer, Cleva, Wise, or bank transfer to accounts in Nigeria, the UK and the USA. Payment details are on your invoice.
