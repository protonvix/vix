Zillow lead conversion

Zillow leads not converting? It's usually the follow-up.

Zillow leads are real people, but many are early in their search and talking to several agents at once. The agents who convert them respond fastest, ask the right first question, and keep following up after everyone else has stopped.

Why Zillow leads feel hard to convert

  • Timing: many are browsing months before they're ready to buy.
  • Competition: they may contact more than one agent; the first helpful reply often wins.
  • Expectations: they asked about one home, not about "working with an agent".
  • Slow or generic replies: a template that ignores the home they asked about gets ignored.

What to do in the first hour

  1. Reply within minutes, by text and call, about the exact home they asked about.
  2. Ask one easy question: "Would you like to see it this week, or are you still exploring the area?"
  3. Offer two tour times or a booking link.
  4. If the home is gone, say so and offer two similar ones. Honesty builds trust fast.

What to do after that

  • Follow up several times in the first week, then move to long-term nurture with matching listings and market updates.
  • Tag the lead source so you can see how many Zillow leads actually close, and how long they take.
  • Review lost leads monthly: was it speed, the first message, or follow-up that dropped off?

Automating Zillow follow-up with GoHighLevel

Zillow leads can be routed into GoHighLevel (usually by email parsing or an integration), so each one triggers an instant personal text, a call task and a booking link, then a nurture sequence if they're not ready. Questions

Quick answers.

Why are my Zillow leads not converting?

Usually slow replies, a generic first message, or follow-up that stops after a few days. Many Zillow leads are early in their search and convert months later with consistent, helpful contact.

How fast should I respond to a Zillow lead?

Within minutes, about the exact home they asked about, with a simple question and an easy way to book a tour.

Can I automate Zillow lead follow-up?

Yes. Leads can be routed into a CRM such as GoHighLevel to trigger an instant text, a call task, a booking link and a long-term nurture sequence.
