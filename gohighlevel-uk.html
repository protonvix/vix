GoHighLevel UK

GoHighLevel for UK businesses, set up the UK way.

Most GoHighLevel guides assume you're in the US. UK businesses need UK numbers, UK consent rules and UK lead sources. Here's what's different and how to set it up properly.

Book a strategy call Check your revenue leak

What's different in the UK

  • No A2P 10DLC. That's a US requirement. UK texting is governed mainly by consent.
  • Consent rules: marketing texts and emails to individuals generally need consent under PECR, and personal data is handled under UK GDPR. Keep a record of how each person opted in.
  • UK numbers: use UK numbers for calls and texts so people recognise and trust them. Some UK setups use an alphanumeric sender name for one-way messages.
  • Spelling and tone: templates written for UK customers (enquiry, viewing, completion, letting).
  • Currency and invoicing in pounds.

UK estate and letting agents

Enquiries from Rightmove, Zoopla and OnTheMarket can be routed into GoHighLevel so every applicant gets a quick reply and a viewing link. Landlords and vendors get their own pipelines, and past clients get regular, useful updates that bring back instructions and referrals.

Other UK businesses

Clinics, trades and home services, coaches and agencies use the same building blocks: fast replies, online booking, reminders, review requests and a clear pipeline.

This page is general information, not legal advice; the ICO publishes guidance on PECR and UK GDPR. For a UK-ready setup, book a free strategy call.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can I use GoHighLevel in the UK?

Yes. GoHighLevel works in the UK with UK phone numbers, email and SMS. Set it up for UK consent rules and UK wording rather than copying a US template.

Is GoHighLevel GDPR compliant?

Compliance depends on how you use it. Collect and record consent, only message people who opted in, honour unsubscribes, and include HighLevel in your privacy policy and data processing records. Check HighLevel's own data protection documents.

Do UK estate agents use GoHighLevel?

Yes, increasingly, for fast replies to portal enquiries, viewing bookings, vendor and landlord follow-up, and past client marketing.
