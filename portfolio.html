How I work

Three steps, every project

Understand the user

Who is visiting, what they need to know and what stops them from acting. I write this down before I open a design tool.

Design the flow

Structure first, then layout, then visual detail. Every screen gets one clear job and one obvious next step.

Build, test, ship

I build it, test it on real phones and desktops, fix what breaks and refine with real feedback.
