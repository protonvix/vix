Questions

Before you hand over the keys.

Do you need my GoHighLevel password?

No. You add me as a team user under Settings, then My Staff, with the permissions you choose. You can remove my access at any time in one click.

What can you actually get done in 10 hours a month?

Typically two or three new workflows, calendar and pipeline upkeep, contact imports and a round of fixes. The free audit tells you exactly which plan fits your account.

Which time zones do you cover?

My working day overlaps the UK in full, the US East Coast in the afternoon and evening, and Australia in the early morning. Urgent issues on the Dedicated plan get same day attention.

Can you work inside my agency's sub accounts under my brand?

Yes. I work white label, never contact your clients unless you ask me to, and sign an NDA before starting.

Do you handle A2P 10DLC registration?

Yes. I prepare the brand and campaign details, submit the registration and handle the follow up questions, so US texting works without carriers blocking your messages.

I don't have GoHighLevel yet. Can you still help?

Yes. I can help you pick the right plan, then set the account up from scratch and move your contacts and tools across.

How do payments and cancellation work?

Plans are billed monthly in advance by card or bank transfer. There is no long contract, so you can cancel before your next billing date and keep everything built in your account.

Do you help with work outside GoHighLevel?

Yes. Your hours can cover inbox and calendar management, customer support, research, social media and website work. See the full list on the virtual assistant services page.
