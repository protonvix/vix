    Before you decide.

    I use Follow Up Boss, kvCORE or another CRM. Is this for me?

    Yes. I can move your leads and follow up into GoHighLevel, or connect your lead sources so GoHighLevel handles speed to lead, nurture and retention while your agents keep the tools they know. The free audit shows which makes more sense.

    How quickly will I see a difference?

    Instant replies and showing reminders are usually live in the first week, so you see faster conversations straight away. Nurture, revival and referral campaigns build results over the following months.

    Is texting my leads in the US compliant?

    I register your A2P 10DLC brand and campaign, add opt out handling and quiet hours, and only message people who enquired or opted in. Your compliance rules always come first.

    Will you talk to my clients?

    Only if you want me to. Every message goes out in your name and is approved by you. I never contact your clients on my own.

    Do I need my GoHighLevel password to give you access?

    No. You add me as a team user with the permissions you choose, and remove access any time in one click.

    What does it cost to try?

    The revenue audit is free and you get a written plan either way. Monthly plans have no long contract, and everything built stays in your account.
