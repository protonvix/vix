FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need my own GoHighLevel account?

Yes. You keep full ownership of your account and data. If you don’t have one yet, I’ll help you choose the right plan and set it up.

How long does a typical build take?

Quick Start builds take about 7 days. Growth Systems take 2–3 weeks, and Agency/SaaS projects usually 3–5 weeks depending on scope.

Can you migrate us from another CRM?

Absolutely. I migrate contacts, pipelines, tags and automations from HubSpot, ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Keap, ActiveCampaign and others, with no lost data.

Will my team know how to use it?

Every project includes recorded Loom walkthroughs and written SOPs. Live team training sessions are available too.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Monthly retainers cover new builds, fixes, optimisation and reporting, with priority response times.

Are you affiliated with HighLevel?

I’m an independent consultant who specialises in the platform. I’m not employed by or officially affiliated with HighLevel, Inc.
